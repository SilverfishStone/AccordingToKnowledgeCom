House rules (rulings made by this app)
  1. A turn is: your cards age one round, then you either skip, or draw and take exactly one action — play one card, use one card, or discard one card (from hand or desk).
  2. Hand-discard costs are configurable per game. When enabled, any printed up-front negative value triggers when the card leaves the hand; cards without a printed hour value do nothing.
  3. A drawn Alert resolves immediately and uses up the turn. Fired! discards your desk at base value only: stored positive hours are lost, while printed negative values and Parasite's full 4h apply. Recession expires every card in play, as printed: stored hours are lost and printed costs are charged again (a house rule makes it a normal discard that banks hours instead). Per-game settings decide whether attached helpers can protect their cards from Alerts.
  4. Negative cards (Risky, Stock Market): the full cost is paid the moment the card enters play. One hour returns to you each round until repaid, then the card earns from the pile. Discarding while hours are still owed forfeits them. Expiring loses everything on the card and charges the base cost again.
  5. Risky jumps to a total of +8 on its 8th round (per its card text), then expires on the 9th.
  6. Professional pays +10 only when discarded on round 8 or later, up to its (possibly extended) deadline. Expiring pays nothing.
  7. Tardy ages the target card one round with no earnings — it can force an immediate expiration.
  8. Unpredictable is worth a fresh value each round, counted from zero: +1, −1, +2, −2… A negative round takes that many hours from your stash (returned next round); discarding while negative forfeits them. A house rule makes the changes pile onto one running total instead.
  9. Weapons can be played on any player, including yourself. (Deadline still needs another player’s card.)
  10. Newbie bins your whole hand with no costs and no effects, then you draw a fresh hand.
  11. Parasite moves 1 hour per round from its holder to its attacker, 4 hours total. Discarding on or before its deadline pays only the balance still showing on it; an immediate discard therefore pays all 4h. If it expires, or remains past its deadline and is later discarded, it resets and pays the attacker the full 4h.
  12. Quit: the attacker picks a visible played card, or "hand" (random card). The game setting decides whether a played card forced out by Quit settles normally or loses its stored hours. A player who loses a hand card refills toward the game’s configured hand size.
  13. Foreign Exchange: you choose which of your own hand cards to give; you receive a random hand card from the target.
  14. Helper timing is configurable per game. Helpers can either be free at any time or restricted to your own turn as its action. Excused can remove an unprotected weapon already in play.
  15. Making room: if a play-weapon lands on a full desk, you discard one of your own cards. The forced-discard banking setting decides whether stored hours settle normally or are lost.
  16. While a weapon sits on your desk, your unprotected non-weapon cards neither earn nor recover owed hours. They still age and can expire. Only Nepotism overrides this freeze.
  17. Performance Review: your hand is discarded under the game’s hand-discard rule, then you redraw toward the configured hand size.
  18. Amnesia rebuilds every card in play as freshly played: earned hours return to the pile, attachments (Extension, Nepotism) are removed, and negative cards re-charge whatever you had recovered.
  19. Extension only adds 5 rounds to a card’s deadline; it does not stop Tardy, Deadline, Quit, desk-weapon freezing, or other instant weapon effects. Nepotism blocks weapons and stops normal expiry. The Alert-protection setting can separately allow attached helpers to block Alerts.
  20. Sharing is Caring earns whenever its linked player's cards earn from the pile. It voids (hours lost) the moment any card of yours or theirs expires.
  21. Hour totals can go negative (tracked as debt, per rules Example 3). Scammer can't steal from a player at 0 or below.
  22. Game end is configurable: a round limit, a goal-hour threshold, or a finite deck. Cards still in play settle as if discarded when the game ends.
