Satellite Courier

How to play

You're a courier satellite running data bounties. Land at a data station with a green light to see its bounty, download the packet, then carry it to the station named in your orders. You're told its sector, then you search for the station.

Bounties pay credits for upgrades and new looks. Mind your fuel, and your speed: nothing coasts forever out here. When fuel runs low, your screen points to the nearest depot within 20 bits.
