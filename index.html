About

A little about the person behind the site.

Hi, I'm Silver. I like understanding how things fit together, and then building small, useful things that make that understanding easier to share.

This site is where those things live. Some are tools you can use right now, some are stories, and others will be articles where I think out loud.

What I care about

  • Learning in public
  • Faith & good-faith conversation
  • Making tools
  • Clear thinking

Goals

What I'm working toward. Updated as things change.

  1. Build tools that make ideas easier to see

    Turn abstract questions into things you can click, drag, and export, starting with the Priorities Chart Maker.

  2. Write regularly and honestly

    Publish stories and articles that show my reasoning, including where I'm unsure or was wrong.

  3. Grow a community of good-faith conversation

    Make a space, through In Good Faith and this site, where people can disagree without contempt.
