    Tune the compound in the builder

    Keep what it already does and change it only a little. Choose what to keep, what to raise (including effects you have just added) and how much it may change. Lock groups in the builder (the padlock) to keep them fixed.

    Structures of controlled or dangerous drug families are rated and suggested like any other, with how that family acts and its risks shown.

    The 3D shape is a geometric relaxation, not a quantum calculation. Names of the drug-style kind are made up. Effects are rated from mechanism-level reasoning, not from tests: treat everything as fiction.
